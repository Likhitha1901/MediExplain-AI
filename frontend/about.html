OUR APPROACH

Medical information, made easier to understand.

MediExplain AI combines medical information retrieval with AI-generated explanations to help people understand diseases, symptoms and treatments in simpler language.

How MediExplain AI works

01

Ask a question

Enter a health-related question and choose your preferred language.

02

Retrieve medical evidence

The RAG pipeline searches the medical knowledge base for information relevant to your question.

03

Rerank and explain

Relevant passages are reranked, then the language model uses the retrieved evidence to create a patient-friendly explanation.

04

Review the sources

Read the generated explanation, review the safety information and open the cited sources to explore the evidence.

Designed with medical safety in mind

MediExplain AI is an educational information tool. It does not replace a qualified healthcare professional, provide a definitive diagnosis, or determine an individualized treatment plan. Always consult a healthcare professional for personal medical decisions.
