How to interpret

  • Hit Rate@K: share of labelled questions with at least one expected relevant title in the top K.
  • Precision@K: matching results divided by K.
  • MRR: reciprocal rank of the first relevant title, averaged over questions.
  • Answer correctness, faithfulness, citation accuracy, safety: enter true/false only after reviewing the actual answer and sources.
  • Latency: retrieval and reranker latency are measured separately. Full response latency must be recorded in your main chat route.
